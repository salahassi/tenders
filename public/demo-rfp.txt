SYNTHETIC RFP — FOR TENDERDESK TESTING ONLY
Reference: DEMO-RFP-2026-001
Buyer: Fictional Civic Services Lab

Scope
The supplier shall deliver a web-based request tracking application for a fictional public-service organization.
The solution must support Arabic and English user-facing content.
The supplier must provide role-based access for administrators, editors, and reviewers.
The solution shall provide an audit record of status and ownership changes.
The supplier must deliver a technical design, implementation plan, test evidence, and user guide.
The supplier must define acceptance criteria and demonstrate the agreed workflows.

Commercial instructions
Provide a separate financial proposal in SAR with the pricing basis, assumptions, exclusions, and tax treatment.
Provide the offer validity period and proposed payment milestones.

Clarifications for the trial
No real customer, employee, source portal, or procurement process is represented.
The delivery schedule, hosting environment, staff rates, and acceptance approver must be confirmed before a proposal is approved.
