# Embedded proposal fonts

Amiri Regular/Bold and Carlito Regular/Bold are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full licenses are included alongside the files. `manifest.json` records the SHA-256 of each bundled font and its embedding flags.

Upstream source: Google Fonts, `ofl/amiri` and `ofl/carlito` at https://github.com/google/fonts. Downloaded from the corresponding `raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/` paths. Amiri metadata identifies version 1.002. These assets are bundled locally; generating an export does not request fonts from a third-party host.

Font licenses do not license or endorse proposal content.
